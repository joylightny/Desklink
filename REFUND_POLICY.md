# DeskBlink Refund Policy

**Effective date:** September 27, 2026

DeskBlink offers a 14-day money-back guarantee for first-time lifetime-license purchases.

## Eligibility

A customer may request a full refund within 14 calendar days of purchase if:

- DeskBlink does not work on the customer’s supported Mac;
- a documented DeskBlink feature does not perform as described; or
- the purchase was made accidentally and the license has not been abused, resold, or redistributed.

## How to request a refund

Email [joylightny@gmail.com](mailto:joylightny@gmail.com) within the eligible period. Include the checkout email address, approximate purchase date, and a short explanation of the problem. Do not email a full payment-card number.

## Processing

Approved refunds are processed by the merchant of record and returned to the original payment method. Bank or card processing times may vary. A refunded DeskBlink license may be disabled when the refund is processed.

## Exceptions

Refunds may be declined when the request is late, or when there is evidence of license-key resale, sharing beyond the two-device limit, fraud, repeated refund abuse, or another material violation of the license terms.

Nothing in this policy limits mandatory consumer rights that apply in the customer’s jurisdiction.

## Contact

Questions about refunds can be sent to [joylightny@gmail.com](mailto:joylightny@gmail.com).
