# DeskBlink Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 27, 2026

This policy explains how DeskBlink handles information when the app is downloaded directly from GitHub and licensed through Lemon Squeezy.

## Local app data

DeskBlink stores app preferences locally on the Mac. Trial and license state may be stored in the macOS Keychain so that the app can remember the trial period and purchased access. DeskBlink does not use the Mac hardware serial number as its license identifier.

## License activation and validation

When a user activates or validates a license, DeskBlink communicates with Lemon Squeezy over HTTPS. The request may include the DeskBlink license key, a randomly generated installation identifier or instance name, the license instance identifier returned by Lemon Squeezy, and information necessary to confirm that the license belongs to DeskBlink.

Lemon Squeezy separately processes checkout, purchaser, payment, tax, refund, fraud-prevention, and license-delivery information under its own terms and privacy practices.

## Permissions

DeskBlink may request macOS Accessibility permission to hide and restore windows reliably. It may request Input Monitoring permission if the user enables global trackpad gestures or mouse-button triggers. These permissions provide user-selected features; they are not used to collect document contents, passwords, browsing history, or typed text.

## Data DeskBlink does not collect

DeskBlink does not sell personal information, serve advertising, create advertising profiles, or collect browsing history. The planned release does not include third-party behavioral analytics.

## Retention and deletion

Local DeskBlink data remains on the Mac until it is removed by the user or operating system. Purchase and license records retained by Lemon Squeezy are governed by Lemon Squeezy’s policies and applicable legal obligations.
